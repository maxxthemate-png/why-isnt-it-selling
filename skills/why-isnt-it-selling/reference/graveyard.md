# The graveyard: 11 tactics that did not survive verification

Twenty patterns went into adversarial refutation, where each was independently re-checked against the
evidence rather than confirmed. **Eleven died.** They are listed here so the cut is auditable and so
nobody quietly reintroduces one.

This list is the most useful page in the free tier. It is easy to find someone who will tell you what to
do. It is hard to find someone who will tell you what they tested and threw away.

**Never recommend anything on this list.** If someone is already relying on one, say so under
"Do not build on" and give the one-line reason.

1. **Weekly, opaque, or retroactive usage caps as the number one cancellation driver.** [CC, churn]
   Refuted 3/3. Real cancellation comments over caps do exist, but the "dominant driver" framing did not
   hold up. Treat cap complaints as one churn signal among several, and see the lock-in-avoidance
   pattern (`#15`, Library tier) for the axis that did survive.
2. **Auto-listing public repos in your directory, then filing a GitHub issue to "claim" it.** [CC,
   marketing] Refuted 2/3.
3. **Outcome-and-refund guarantee with a hard deadline, pinned everywhere.** [TA, monetization]
   Refuted 3/3.
4. **Multi-hour "full courses" as evergreen top-of-funnel.** [TA, marketing] Refuted 3/3.
5. **Affiliate links with named creator promo codes.** [TA/CC, monetization] Refuted 3/3.
6. **README as a conversion asset (trust badges, testimonials, funnel numbers).** [CC, marketing]
   Refuted 3/3.
7. **Rewriting copy from feature terms to the one pain or fear removed.** [TA, marketing] Refuted 3/3.
8. **Reducing churn by embedding into the customer's systems and doing setup for them.** [TA, churn]
   Refuted 3/3.
9. **Value-based or outcome pricing, and the free-audit to retainer ladder.** [TA/CC, monetization]
   Refuted 2/3.
10. **App-store optimization (keyword in the name, distinctive first screenshot, review modal at the
    emotional peak).** [TA, marketing/churn] Refuted 3/3.
11. **Launching paid from day zero (free trial, never freemium).** [TA, monetization] Refuted 3/3.

**That verification stage is the point, not overhead. 11 of 20 draft patterns were confident-sounding
and wrong.**

## What "refuted" means here

`Refuted 3/3` means three independent re-checks each failed to sustain the claim. `Refuted 2/3` means
two of three did.

It does not mean the tactic never works for anybody. It means the evidence that looked like a pattern
did not survive someone actively trying to break it, so it is not a basis to build distribution on. If
you already do one of these and it demonstrably works for you, your own data outranks this list.
